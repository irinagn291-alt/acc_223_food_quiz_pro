Food Quiz is an iPhone app for testing food and nutrition knowledge using real packaged products from the Open Food Facts database.

• Play five game modes: Which Has More?, Guess the Country, Guess the Brand, Guess Calories, and Order by Calories
• In Which Has More?, compare Protein, Calories, Fat, Carbohydrates, Sugar, or Fiber between two products
• In Guess the Country, identify a product's origin country from its image and name
• In Guess the Brand, pick the correct brand from a product image
• In Guess Calories, estimate kcal per 100g from multiple choices
• In Order by Calories, drag four products into order from highest to lowest calories
• Scan barcodes with the camera or Choose from Gallery to look up a product
• Search by name, brand, or barcode and open Product Details
• Product Details shows Nutri-Score, NOVA, nutrition per 100g, ingredients, and allergens
• First-launch onboarding can be skipped with Skip or completed with Continue and Get Started

Discover food facts and practice nutrition knowledge with real products.
