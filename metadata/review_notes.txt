Food Quiz is an iPhone app for people who want to test food and nutrition knowledge with real packaged products. It offers five quiz game modes and tools to scan or search products and view nutrition details from Open Food Facts. No account or credentials are required. On a cold launch, the app shows onboarding with four screens (Welcome to Food Quiz, Play 5 Game Modes, Scan Any Product, Search and Explore). The reviewer can tap Skip or swipe through and tap Continue, then Get Started to reach the main tab bar with Games, Search, and Scanner. On Games, tap any mode to play: Which Has More?, Guess the Country, Guess the Brand, Guess Calories, or Order by Calories. On Search, enter a product name, brand, or barcode and submit to view results and open Product Details. On Scanner, tap Start Scanning to use the camera on a product barcode, or tap Choose from Gallery to pick a photo containing a barcode; a successful lookup opens Product Details. The app contacts Open Food Facts at https://world.openfoodfacts.org for product lookup by barcode (/api/v2/product/{barcode}.json), text search (/cgi/search.pl), and random products by category for games. Product images are loaded from URLs returned by Open Food Facts. On the device, the app stores whether onboarding is completed and recently played game modes in local app storage, and caches downloaded product images in the app caches directory. App behavior does not differ by region; there is no region-specific logic in the code. No gambling, alcohol licensing, or other regulated-industry paperwork applies; the app is an educational food quiz and product lookup tool with no accounts, payments, or regulated services.
