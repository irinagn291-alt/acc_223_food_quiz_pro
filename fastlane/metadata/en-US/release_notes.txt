Welcome to Food Quiz 1.0.

This initial release includes:

• Onboarding with four screens introducing games, scanning, and search
• Games tab with Which Has More?, Guess the Country, Guess the Brand, Guess Calories, and Order by Calories
• Scanner tab with Start Scanning, Choose from Gallery, and product lookup
• Search tab to find products by name, brand, or barcode
• Product Details with Nutri-Score, NOVA, nutrition per 100g, ingredients, and allergens
• Product data and images from Open Food Facts
